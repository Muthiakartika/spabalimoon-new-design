P: Balinese Massage is often recommended for first time guests because it combines relaxation, gentle stretching, and traditional massage techniques suitable for most people.
H3: 4. Can I combine different treatments in one visit?

P: Tell us how you're feeling or what you'd like to achieve, whether that's relaxation, muscle recovery, skin care, or simply time to unwind. We'll happily recommend the most suitable treatment for you.
H3: 6. Is Spa Bali Moon a licensed spa?
