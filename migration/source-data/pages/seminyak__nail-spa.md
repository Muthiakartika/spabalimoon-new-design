H2: Everything You Need to Know
H3: Is Gel Nail Art better than regular nail polish?
P: Gel Nail Art generally lasts longer and keeps its shine better than traditional nail polish, making it a popular choice for holidays and special occasions.
H3: Can I bring my own nail design reference?
P: Yes. You're welcome to show inspiration photos so our nail artists can recreate a similar style that suits your nails.
H3: Will gel nails damage my natural nails?
P: When applied and removed correctly, gel products can be used while maintaining the health of your natural nails. Our team follows gentle application techniques throughout the treatment.
H3: How long does a Nail Art appointment take?
P: The duration depends on the design complexity, but most appointments take between 45 and 90 minutes.
H3: How long will Gel Nail Art last?
P: Many guests enjoy beautiful results for around two to three weeks, depending on nail growth and daily activities.
H3: What is nail art?
P: Nail art is the decoration of fingernails or toenails using colours, patterns, designs, or other decorative details. It can be added to natural nails or applied over gel or polish.
