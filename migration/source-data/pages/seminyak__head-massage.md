H3: Can a Head Massage help with stress?
P: Many guests choose Head Massage because the slow, repetitive techniques promote relaxation and help reduce feelings of physical and mental tension.

H3: Can Head Massage help after long hours at a computer?
P: Yes. Prolonged screen time often creates tension around the neck, shoulders, and scalp. Head Massage focuses on these areas to improve overall comfort.
H3: Is Head Massage suitable before sleeping?
P: Many guests enjoy receiving a Head Massage in the evening because the relaxing techniques help the body unwind before rest.
H3: Can I receive Head Massage at my hotel or villa?
