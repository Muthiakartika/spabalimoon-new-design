## Rules applied

- **Original Spa Bali Moon images only.** No stock, AI or placeholder images will be added. Every photo below comes from spabalimoon.com.
- **Step 6 completed on 2026-09-23:** every image marked "✅ MIGRATED" was downloaded from production and saved at the listed path. Each copy was checked with an MD5 hash against the downloaded original. **385 files** migrated (≈20 MB) · **20** byte-identical duplicates reuse an already migrated file · **69** intentionally not migrated (40 theme shapes, 29 `-sm` downscales, 1 placeholder; the reason is in each row). The machine-readable old → new mapping is in `source-data/image-map.json`.
- Folder note: `/images/home/` and `/images/homepage/` on the old site contain **different** photos with the same names (`homepage-2/3/5.webp`). So `/images/homepage/*` goes to `public/images/home/shared/` and nothing is overwritten.
- **Filenames are kept** (lower-cased and kebab-cased only where needed, e.g. `lymphaticmassage .webp` with a space becomes `lymphaticmassage.webp`) so every new file can be traced back to its old URL. Folders follow the requested structure: `home/`, `treatments/`, `beauty/`, `packages/`, `gallery/`, `blog/`, `branding/`. Page-specific photos sit in a sub-folder named after the page's URL slug (e.g. `public/images/treatments/balinese-massage/`), so a beginner can find a page's images from its URL.
- **Not migrated:** decorative theme shapes (old visual design), `-sm` mobile copies (next/image makes responsive sizes), and one grey placeholder image. Each is listed with its reason.
- **Orientation guidance for the redesign:**
  - Hero backgrounds `*-1.webp` are **1920×850 landscape**: use them for full-width heroes with `object-fit: cover`.
  - Treatment cards `listmenu/*` are **630×580 near-square**: use them for cards and price list thumbnails.
  - Homepage catalog photos `homepage-6…27` are **233×306 portrait and low resolution**: show them only at small sizes (≤ 300 px wide). Do not stretch them into landscape.
  - Homepage hero photos `homepage-1` (388×561) and `homepage-2` (337×452) are **small portraits**. The redesign hero should use a 1920 px landscape original (e.g. `homepage-5`, `homepage-28`, `pricelist-1`, `outcall-5`, `contact-1`), or these portraits in a small editorial split. See migration-audit IMG-02.
- **Duplicates:** 21 byte-identical pairs exist (e.g. `listmenu/balinesemassage.webp` = `services/balinesemassage/balinesemassage-3.webp`). Each pair is migrated once and marked "DUPLICATE".
